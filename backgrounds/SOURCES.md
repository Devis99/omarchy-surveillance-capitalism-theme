# Wallpaper sources

All twelve wallpapers are original to this theme and drawn at 3840×2160 with Pillow,
set in CaskaydiaMono with faint scanlines and grain. They come in pairs: a title card,
then the same sentence taken literally, with one subject in the centre.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `01-surveillance-capitalism.jpg` | Title card |
| `02-observation-continuous.jpg` | Title card |
| `03-data-points.jpg` | The subject made only of the data collected about him |
| `04-raw-material.jpg` / `05-extraction.jpg` | Card, then the subject breaking up into extracted data |
| `06-being-predicted.jpg` / `07-next-position.jpg` | Card, then a woman walking toward a lit shop (AI-generated illustration base, upscaled and gradient-mapped into the palette), her three predicted steps and the sponsored destination drawn on top |
| `08-nothing-to-hide.jpg` / `09-contents-indexed.jpg` | Card, then a see-through man in the same illustrated style (AI-generated base, upscaled and gradient-mapped), with his phone, card, wallet, watch, earbuds and heart rate boxed where they really are |
| `10-sanctuary-not-found.jpg` / `11-transparent-walls.jpg` | Card, then an illustrated cutaway house at night (AI-generated base, upscaled and gradient-mapped) with every device in the room boxed and reporting out |
| `12-status-irrelevant.jpg` | Title card |

Walls 07, 09 and 11 are built on AI-generated base images (OpenAI image generation via
Codex); the data figure and the extraction wall use an AI-generated silhouette as a
stencil. Every box, label and line is drawn on top at full resolution. Every subject is a
different ordinary person.

The classification colours follow the idea of *Person of Interest*'s machine
interface, and the captions paraphrase concepts from Shoshana Zuboff's *The Age of
Surveillance Capitalism*. No frames from the show and no text from the book are
included.
